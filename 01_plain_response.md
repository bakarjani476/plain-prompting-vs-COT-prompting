# Run 1 — Plain Prompt Response

**Prompt:** A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

**Response:**

The ball costs $0.10.

**Note:** This is the classic incorrect answer. If the ball were $0.10, the bat would have to cost $1.10 (to be $1.00 more), making the total $1.20 — not $1.10. The answer fails to satisfy the problem's own conditions.
